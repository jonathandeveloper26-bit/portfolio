About

From workshop to web

Mechanical engineer, educator, now developer. Here is how those three fit together.

Who I am

I trained as a mechanical engineer at the Colorado School of Mines and spent my first years in safety-critical naval environments. In 2021 I moved into international education with Village Schools International, where I ended up designing the financial, HR and academic systems that 93 schools rely on.

That work taught me what I enjoy most: taking a messy, manual process and building the tool that makes it reliable. It's why I'm now at Epitech Paris, turning that instinct into a proper software engineering foundation.

I'm looking for a six-month internship from January to June 2027, under a convention de stage.
